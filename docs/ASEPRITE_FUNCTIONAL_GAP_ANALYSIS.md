# Aseprite vs SpriteRepair — Functional Gap Analysis & Decision Matrix

**Audit Date**: 2026-09-10  
**Purpose**: Rigorous feature-by-feature comparison between standard Aseprite v1.3.18.5 capabilities and current SpriteRepair implementation, establishing explicit `ADOPT`, `ADAPT`, and `IGNORE` architectural decisions with development priorities (`P0`–`P3`).

---

## 2. Strategic Differentiation: SpriteRepair Superpowers

While Aseprite excels at manual pixel art illustration, **SpriteRepair's unique value proposition lies in AI and Computer Vision repair automation** that Aseprite completely lacks:

1. **AI Grid Seed vs Hard Cells**: AI spritesheets frequently have floating limbs or slashes breaking cell boundaries. SpriteRepair's soft-flood extraction automatically rescues overflow rather than clipping it.
2. **Automated Foot / Root Normalization**: Calculates ground contact and root center of mass automatically, stabilizing wobbling AI characters to `stdev < 1.0px`.
3. **Character vs VFX Layer Separation**: Distinguishes between solid body mass and transient particle effects, preventing VFX explosions from shifting the character's root position.
4. **Neighbor Contamination Recovery**: The Ownership Mask brush allows users to resolve overlapping adjacent frames with single-click subtraction or alpha retrieval.
5. **AI Vision Animation QA**: Integrates vision LLMs (NVIDIA Kimi K3 / Gemini Flash Lite) to inspect the contact sheet, identifying pose continuity breaks, bad frames, and scale anomalies before game engine import.
